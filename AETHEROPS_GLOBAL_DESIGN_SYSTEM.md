# AETHEROPS GLOBAL DESIGN SYSTEM

## 1. Typography Hierarchy
- **Main Page Title**: 26–30px (`clamp(1.625rem, 2.5vw, 1.875rem)`), Semibold (600).
- **Page Description**: 16px (`1rem`), Regular (400), secondary text.
- **Section Heading**: 21–23px (`clamp(1.3125rem, 2vw, 1.4375rem)`), Semibold (600).
- **Card Heading**: 18px (`1.125rem`), Semibold (600).
- **Body Text**: 16px (`1rem`) mobile, 15px (`0.9375rem`) desktop, Regular (400).
- **Secondary Text**: 14px (`0.875rem`), Regular (400).
- **Labels**: 14–15px (`0.875rem` - `0.9375rem`), Medium (500).
- **Metric Values**: 34–40px (`2.125rem` - `2.5rem`), Bold (700).
- **Button & Input Text**: 16px (`1rem`), Medium (500).

---

## 2. Component Sizing & Spacing Standards
- **Header Heights**: Mobile: 64px; Desktop: 72px.
- **Button & Input Heights**: 48px–52px (`h-12` to `h-13`), ensuring touch target compliance.
- **Icon Buttons**: Minimum 44px touch area.
- **Card Padding**: 20px–24px (`p-5` to `p-6`).
- **Section Spacing**: 28px–32px gap.
- **Border Radius**: Cards & Modals: 16px–18px (`rounded-2xl`). Buttons & Inputs: 12px (`rounded-xl`).
